title=Blind Sound Hunters
description=[h1]Blind Sound Hunters[/h1]
description=
description=[b]The zombies can't see you. They can hear everything.[/b]
description=
description=Every zombie in the world is completely blind. They hunt by sound alone: noise makes them sprint toward the source, silence leaves them shambling aimlessly, and only physical contact lets them lock onto you.
description=
description=Stand still and quiet, and a horde can shuffle right past you. Fire a gun, smash a window or start a car, and every zombie in earshot comes running.
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=Zombies switch between three behaviors:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Wandering (blind)[/b] – Zombies shamble around slowly, at random. They cannot see you, even when you are right in front of them.
description=[*][b]Alerted (sprinting)[/b] – When a zombie hears a sound, it turns into a sprinter and runs to the spot where the sound came from. Louder and closer sounds take priority. (You can make them walk instead of sprint in the Sandbox options.) When it reaches the spot, or after a while without new sounds, it goes back to shambling.
description=[*][b]Tactile combat[/b] – If a zombie bumps into you, or you hit or shove it, that zombie (and only that one) locks onto you and attacks. To lose it, get some distance and stay completely silent for a moment.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]What makes noise[/h2]
description=Anything that makes a sound in the game: gunshots, car engines and horns, sirens, alarms, breaking windows, generators, running footsteps, shouting and more. The bigger the sound, the farther it reaches.
description=
description=Zombie groans and thumping do [b]not[/b] alert other zombies. Only real noise sources do.
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=All settings are on the [b]Blind Sound Hunters[/b] page when you create a new Sandbox game:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Hearing Multiplier[/b] (default 1.0) – Multiplies how far every sound reaches.
description=[*][b]Minimum Sound Radius[/b] (default 5) – Sounds smaller than this (in tiles) are ignored.
description=[*][b]Zombie Strength[/b] (default Superhuman) – Strength of every zombie: Superhuman, Normal or Weak.
description=[*][b]Sprint Toward Sounds[/b] (default on) – Turn it off and alerted zombies only walk toward sounds instead of sprinting.
description=[*][b]Alert Memory[/b] (default 15 s) – How long an alerted zombie keeps chasing a sound before giving up.
description=[*][b]Silence to Escape[/b] (default 2 s) – How long you must stay silent and at a distance to escape a zombie in tactile combat.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Tips[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Silence is your best weapon. Sneak, walk and avoid running near hordes.
description=[*]Use noise as a tool: a car alarm or a broken window can pull a horde away from where you want to go.
description=[*]Be careful with melee. Every hit locks that zombie onto you.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Made for [b]Build 42[/b] (tested on 42.22).
description=[*]Tested in [b]singleplayer[/b] only. Multiplayer is not supported yet.
description=[*]It replaces vanilla zombie sight and hearing, so it may conflict with other mods that change zombie AI, vision or hearing.
description=[*]Safe to add to an existing save. Removing it returns zombies to vanilla behavior.
description=[/list]
