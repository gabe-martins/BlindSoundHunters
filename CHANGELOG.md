# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release, for Project Zomboid Build 42 (tested on 42.22.2, singleplayer).

- Blind zombies: they cannot see the player at all.
- Three states: wandering, alerted (sprinting toward a sound) and tactile combat (locked onto the player after touch, a hit or a shove).
- Sound filtering with a ring buffer, hearing multiplier, minimum sound radius, alert memory and silence-to-escape.
- Sandbox options page with English and Brazilian Portuguese translations.
